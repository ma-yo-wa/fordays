# Fordays ethos

**Fordays** — *Plans for days.*

Fordays is for two people who want to plan life together without feeling like they’re using a calendar app.

---

## Simpler than a calendar

Every screen should be **ridiculously simple** — easier than Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.

| Calendars ask | Fordays asks |
|---|---|
| Exact start, end, timezone, busy/free, recurrence | Do we have a **day** yet? |
| Fill every field to save | Leave things blank; soft is fine |
| Mode switchers (month / week / day) | One composition: month map + day list |
| Meeting chrome | Plan language: bucket, day, From, Until |

**Test:** Could someone who hates calendars finish the flow without thinking about duration, invites, or free/busy? If not, simplify.

---

## When a plan gets a day

Default path (bucket → plan, new plan, change the day, suggest):

1. **Day** — required once it’s a plan  
2. **From** — optional  
3. **Until** — optional  

That’s enough for all day, “around 7,” or “7–9.”

**Multi-day is progressive disclosure** — not a third always-on field:

- Quiet control: **Runs more than one day?**  
- Then **Ends on** (last day)  
- **Until** means the time on that last day (still optional)  
- Cue when multi-day is open: *Until is on the last day*

Unknown end is normal. Never block save because Until or Ends on is empty.

---

## Language

- Say **plan**, **day**, **bucket** — not “put it on the calendar,” “schedule,” or “event.”  
- **Make it a plan** / **Change the day** / **Back to the bucket list**  
- Pastels never take white text; ink on fills. Orb palette: rose = plan energy, sage = idea.

---

## Surfaces

- Product lives on the **phone** (iOS + PWA for now).  
- Desktop browsers don’t run the planner.  
- Marketing can live on the web; the app does not.

Ship the same product behavior on **PWA and iOS** together.
